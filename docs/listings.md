## Uzun açıklama

Simple Trading Journal is a web-based trading journal for forex, stock, crypto and commodities traders — including people trading prop firm accounts.

**Trades come in by themselves.** Install the expert advisor on MetaTrader 4 or 5 once and every closed trade lands in your journal, along with a year of history. No MetaTrader? Upload the report your broker gives you — MT5, MT4, cTrader, TradeLocker, DXtrade and Match-Trader files are recognised, unfamiliar CSVs can be mapped by hand, and re-uploading the same file only adds new trades.

**Notes you'll actually write.** Dictate your pre- and post-trade notes out loud; the text tidies itself and knows trading terms like Order Block, FVG and CHoCH. Attach before and after screenshots.

**Numbers instead of memory.** Win rate, expectancy, profit factor, payoff ratio, drawdown and streaks, a P&L calendar, heat map by hour and day, and performance per setup. An optional AI review reads your whole history and points out strengths and leaks.

**Discipline, measured.** Your own pre-trade checklists, monthly targets, maximum risk and no-trade hours. The discipline view flags revenge trades, overtrading, raising the stake and trading outside your usual hours — all from data you already entered.

**Built for prop accounts.** Set the firm's profit target, daily loss and total drawdown (static or trailing) and see where the account stands. A prop account review scores a firm's rules on 12 criteria.

**Private by default.** Each user's data is isolated with row-level security. The app works in English, Turkish, Persian, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.

The Free plan is permanent: 1 journal, 2 trades a day (extra trades are kept, not deleted), MetaTrader sync, imports, all statistics and the calendar. Pro can be tried for 3 days without a card.
